# Kinship

A guild companion for **WoW Forever** that rewards everything that makes a guild a
guild — not just raid loot. Think DKP with a much wider scope.

> Status: early design. Nothing playable yet.

## Idea

- **Deeds are recorded automatically**: boss kills, dungeon clears, time and XP earned
  while grouped with guildies, helping lower-level guildies, crafting for guildies,
  resurrections, raid attendance, and more.
- **Points and ranks**: deeds earn points according to rules the guild leader sets
  in game. Every member's client computes the same balances.
- **Raid loot auctions**: bid with points, checked against each bidder's balance.
- **Officer tools**: corrections and adjustments, kudos, bank donations, sign-ups.
- **No website, no external app**: data syncs directly between guild members'
  clients over addon messages, so anyone online can bring a returning player up to date.
- **Works for any guild**: no hard-coded guild names; rules are configured in game.
